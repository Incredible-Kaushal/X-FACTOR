Workout Schedules

Split Type Structure Pros cons
Bro Split 1 muscle group per day (e.g., Chest, Back, Shoulders, Arms, Legs) High focus on each muscle, fun for bodybuilding Lower frequency (muscle hit only once a week)
Push/Pull/Legs (PPL) Push (chest, shoulders, triceps), Pull (back, biceps), Legs Balanced, higher frequency (2× per week if repeated) Can be tiring if done 6 days/week
Upper/Lower Split Upper body one day, lower body next Simple, effective for strength & hypertrophy Less isolation, more compound focus
Full Body Split Train all major muscles in one session Great for beginners, efficient Sessions can be long, recovery demanding
